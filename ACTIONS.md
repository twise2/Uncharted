#### Claim Resources

At the start of each turn, raise each of your resource counters by the number of total resources found on planets you control with factories (Max of 10).

## NAVIGATE

#### Chart

At any time on your turn, you may pay 1 amalgamite to place a hex on any undiscovered location on the map adjacent to any hex you occupy.

#### Move

Ships may only move one hex per turn. Choose an occupied hex and another adjacent hex or empty space. Move as many ships as you want from one hex to the other. If moving to an undiscovered space, roll a d20. On any roll under 5, destroy the ship. On any other roll, place a hex on the location you moved the ship.

#### Warp

Only use after attacking is concluded for the turn. Pay 1 Anti-Matter from your store. Move a ship any distance from one hex to any other hex that can be reached in a straight line that does not go through a gravity well, star, or black hole. If moving from a warp gate location, you may move any number of ships with a maximum cost of 5 anti-matter. If moving from a hex with a warp gate to another hex with a warp gate ignore all cost and location limitations. When moving from a warp gate to a warp gate on an occupied hex, you must get permission from a player who is occupying that hex.

#### Combat

Any time on your turn, you may start combat by choosing a hex with more than one player occupying it.

You then declare one or more players you wish to attack and you and that player are considered opted-in for combat. Next, any other players the hex may choose to opt-in to combat declaring a side they want to fight on.

Each opted-in player then rolls a number of 20-sided dice equal to the amount of ships they contain on the hex. Any player with a shipyard on the hex adds 2 to each of their rolls and rolls 2 additional die.

For each roll a player rolls above a 10, that player may then remove one ship or shipyard (with ships having to be removed first) from the hex. For every die rolled, players may spend 1 adamantine to negate the die. the player who's die it is, may then spend 1 anti-matter to negate the negation.

At this time if only one player maintains any ships or shipyard, that player is condsidered the victor. If no victor is declared players have the option to:

-Retreat by moving all of their ships off the hex to the nearest unoccupied hex. Then players have the option to call a truce, going back to the state (Note: if retreating, a shipyard is considered abandoned and is automatically destroyed)
-Declare a truce, if all players remaining do not wish to continue combat, combat may end and all ships stay on the hex. Players can in trade negotiates.

After an attack, the vicotrious players may then replace any remaining tokens on that hex with their own without paying build cost. (Note: If multiple players declare a truce after defeating a player who's tokens exist, they must choose owners of the new factory before combat ends)

## Build

#### Build Factory

Remove 5 adamantine, and 5 dark-matter, and 5 anti-matter from your store. Place a factory token on any planet you occupy that doesn't currently have a factory.

#### Build Shipyard

Remove 5 adamantine, and 5 anti-matter from your store. Place a shipyard token on any occupied planet that has atleast one moon. You may only have one shipyard per hex.

#### Build Ship

Remove 1 adamantine, 1 anti-matter, and 1 amalgamite from your store. Build a ship on any location where you have a shipyard.

#### Build Warp Gate

Remove 5 anti-matter, 5 dark-matter, and 5 adamantine from your store. Place a warp gate token on any planet you occupy with rings.

#### Build a Gravity Well

Remove 1 dark matter from your store. Place a gravity Well token on any edge of any hex you occupy.

#### TRADE

You may trade resources from your store with any other player who occupies a space adjacent to one of your occupied space. At any time, you may also trade in 4 of any resource for 1 of any other resource.
